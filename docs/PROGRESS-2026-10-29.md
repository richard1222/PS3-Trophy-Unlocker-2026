## What changed

- Installed PS3 digital-game discovery now includes titles whose trophy lists are not initialized.
- Valid PS1/PS2 Classics are filtered before enforcing the PS3 installation-ID match.
- An explicit registered-only review lists each excluded digital title. Excluded games receive no trophies in that job.
- The selected scope is checked during the job; changed or incomplete scope stops work.
- Matching setup, preparation consent and final award consent remain separate.

Presence of a trophy archive, or a “Demo”/“Core Fighters” label, does not by itself establish whether a game should register trophies. No unsupported-game or license-cause claim is made from those labels.
